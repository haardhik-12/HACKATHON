# CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) Resources

## Core CBT Skills
Based on evidence-backed research for anxiety and depression management.

### Cognitive Restructuring
The process of identifying and challenging automatic negative thoughts (ANTs).
- **Identify**: Recognize the thought as it occurs.
- **Examine**: Look for evidence for and against the thought.
- **Balanced Alternative**: Develop a more realistic and helpful perspective.

### Behavioral Activation
Increasing engagement in positive, mood-lifting activities to break the cycle of depression.
- **Scheduling**: Planning activities that provide a sense of pleasure or achievement.
- **Monitoring**: Tracking the relationship between activity and mood.

### Goal Setting and Distortion Identification
NHS-aligned self-help exercises.
- **S.M.A.R.T. Goals**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.
- **Cognitive Distortions**: Identifying common patterns like catastrophizing, all-or-nothing thinking, and emotional reasoning.

### Thought Records
A systematic way to record and evaluate situations, feelings, and underlying beliefs.
