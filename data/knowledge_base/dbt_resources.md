# DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy) Resources

## DBT Skills for Emotional Wellness
A framework for managing intense emotions and improving interpersonal relationships.

### Mindfulness
The foundational skill of being fully present in the moment without judgment.
- **Observe**: Just noticing internal and external experiences.
- **Describe**: Putting words to what is observed.
- **Participate**: Engaging fully in the current activity.

### Distress Tolerance
Getting through a crisis without making it worse.
- **TIP Skills**: Temperature (cold water), Intense exercise, Paced breathing.
- **Self-Soothing**: Engaging the five senses (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch).
- **Distraction**: Activities to shift focus during acute distress.

### Emotion Regulation
Reducing vulnerability to the "emotion mind" and increasing positive experiences.
- **Check the Facts**: Seeing if emotions fit the actual situation.
- **Opposite Action**: Acting contrary to an unhelpful emotional urge.

### Interpersonal Effectiveness
Maintaining relationships and self-respect while asking for what you need.
- **DEAR MAN**: Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, Mindful, Appear confident, Negotiate.
- **GIVE**: Gentle, Interested, Validate, Easy manner.
