# Clinical Guidelines for Mental Health

## NICE Guidelines (CG123)
Common mental health disorders: identification and pathways to care.

### Overview:
The NICE Guideline CG123 provides evidence-based stepped-care pathways for identifying and managing common mental health disorders like depression and anxiety in primary care. 

### Key Emphasis:
- **Stepped-Care Model**: Providing the least intrusive, most effective intervention first.
- **Access**: Improving access to psychological therapies (IAPT).
- **Psychoeducation**: Ensuring patients and families are informed about conditions and treatment options.
- **Identification**: Early screening and assessment in primary care settings.

### Management Pathways:
- Step 1: Identification, assessment, and psychoeducation.
- Step 2: Low-intensity interventions (self-help, group-based CBT).
- Step 3: High-intensity interventions (individual CBT, IPT).

## American Psychiatric Association (APA) Guidelines
Professional practice guidelines for psychiatric evaluation and treatment.

### Key Focus Areas:
- **Psychiatric Evaluation**: Standards for comprehensive patient assessment.
- **Evidence-Based Assessment**: Using validated tools and criteria for diagnosis.
- **Treatment Planning**: Developing personalized plans based on clinical evidence.
- **Periodic Review**: Regularly updating assessments and plans based on patient progress.
