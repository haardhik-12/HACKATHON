# Mental Health Clinical Guidelines & Support Summary

## NICE Guidelines (CG123) Summary
The NICE guidelines (CG123) provide a stepped-care model for managing common mental health disorders like depression and anxiety. 
- **Step 1**: Identification, assessment, and psychoeducation.
- **Step 2**: Low-intensity interventions (e.g., self-help, group-based CBT).
- **Step 3**: High-intensity interventions (e.g., individual CBT, IPT).
The focus is on early identification and providing the least intrusive yet effective intervention first.

## Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Core Skills
CBT focus includes cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation.
- **Cognitive Restructuring**: Identifying and challenging automatic negative thoughts (ANTs).
- **Behavioral Activation**: Increasing engagement in positive, mood-lifting activities.
- **Thought Records**: Systematically recording situations, feelings, and thoughts to find balanced alternatives.

## Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) Skills
DBT provides specific skills for emotional intensity:
- **Mindfulness**: Being fully present in the moment without judgment.
- **Distress Tolerance**: Getting through a crisis without making it worse (e.g., TIP skills, self-soothing).
- **Emotion Regulation**: Reducing vulnerability to "emotion mind" and increasing positive emotional experiences.
- **Interpersonal Effectiveness**: Asking for what you need and saying no while maintaining self-respect and relationships.

## Emotional Well-being
Emotional well-being is defined as a positive state characterized by resilience, self-acceptance, and the ability to manage life's stresses effectively. Interventions include positive psychology exercises and stress reduction techniques.
